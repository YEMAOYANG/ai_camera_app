Even grid with nine cells; generous completely transparent gutters and edges. Center one full subject per cell, never touching another cell. No props unless specified, shadows, background color, lines, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks. Matte gouache, fine paper grain, clean soft cut-paper edges, warm cream, pine green, apricot and honey colors, whole silhouettes legible at tiny display size.
