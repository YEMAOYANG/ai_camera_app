WHAT A TEACHER DOES (examples from the pilot; follow the moves, write your own words for your lesson)
- A concept slide (8-10 lines): hook the child into a small story or situation; show and name the idea in child language with the picture ("看这个货篮，里面装了不少东西，我们来数数苹果有几个"); a classmate asks the child's own question ("小满问：苹果有几个呀？"); another answers or counts with the child ("朵朵轻声说：1、2、3，有3个！"); STATE THE RULE in one clear sentence ("数到3，就用数字3来表示"; "一样多用等于号；左边多，开口向左，用大于号"); a rhyme or memory phrase when the subject has one ("两队站好比一比，一一对应看仔细。一样多用等于号，大口朝着多的那边"); the owl sums up the takeaway ("猫头鹰站长提醒我们：数到几，就用数字几来表示。记住这个好办法"); a closing question the child carries to the next page.
- An interactive page (8-12 lines): frame the task in one or two lines; THE TEACHER DEMONSTRATES one complete example with the page's operations while saying why at each step ("先不急着选符号，我们先数一数。左边有3只小鸭，1、2、3。右边有4根胡萝卜，1、2、3、4。3比4少，所以3小于4"); say the rule the demonstration shows; then hand over ("现在轮到你啦，先数一数两边，再选一个符号") with one practical tip ("点一个数一个；点快了就点重置重数"). The child's own attempt is silent: the page gives its own feedback, so the narration happens before the child plays.
- A quiz scene: the `intro` lines tell the child what is coming and remind of the one rule that helps ("听老师读题，想一想我们刚学的办法，再看图选一选"); never give an answer.
- The last slide recaps the knowledge points of the whole lesson (3-5 rule sentences) with the owl and a classmate, and sends the child home with one thing to look for in daily life.
Every page must teach something new or practise it with reasons; no page is only a transition.
