# Mira project instructions

Apply the shared project instructions:

@AGENTS.md

For courseware authoring, review or revision, the single standard is the
following document. Read it completely before planning or changing a lesson:

@docs/learning/courseware-standard.md

The earlier interaction policy files, the workbench job freezing contract
(`scopeReview`, `pagePlan`, `sceneContract`, the compact author dispatch) and
the per-lesson native checks described in older notes belong to lessons that are
already published. They are superseded for new work: do not apply them, and
never overwrite the frozen requests, receipts or review records of a published
lesson to make it look as if it followed the new standard. To repair a
published lesson, publish a new version and let the release retire the old one.

Generated speech, pictures and pages are candidates until the release gates pass
and a person has played them. A passing gate does not prove that the voice is
pleasant, the game is fun or the child has learned; report the gates, actual
play, audio and student-entry checks separately.
